Privacy Policy

Raha Restaurant runs a simple static website. We do not have user accounts, and we do not collect or store personal data on this site.

What stays on your device

  • Your cart is saved in your browser's local storage only.
  • Clearing the cart or your browser data removes it completely.

WhatsApp and phone orders

When you tap "Send order on WhatsApp", your cart is turned into a message that you choose to send. From that point the conversation is handled inside WhatsApp under their terms, and we use your name, phone number and order details only to prepare and confirm your order.
